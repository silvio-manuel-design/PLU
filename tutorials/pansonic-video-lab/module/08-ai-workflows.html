Module 08

AI Workflows

AI is not a replacement for creativity — it's a multiplier. Those who strategically integrate AI into their video production save hours of work and gain time for what truly matters: The story.

AI as a Productivity Multiplier

The biggest lie about AI is that it takes over the work. The truth: AI handles the repetitive, time-consuming parts — so you have more time for creativity, decisions, and human details. A video that used to take a day is ready in three hours with AI support.

80% faster editing AI-powered transcription and auto-cut save up to 80% of editing time.
90% less noise cleanup AI noise suppression handles in seconds what used to take minutes.
3× more content With AI workflows, you produce three times as many videos in the same time.
Indoor Tip

For tutorials, AI transcription is gold: automatic subtitles, chapter markers and show notes generated from a single video. Tools like Whisper or Descript transcribe 30 minutes of video in under 2 minutes.

Outdoor Tip

Outdoor videos benefit massively from AI stabilization and auto-reframe. A shaky handheld shot becomes a smooth gimbal look — done in software afterward, without expensive equipment.

Hybrid Tip

AI-based face recognition and speaker tracking keep you in focus — whether indoor interview or outdoor walk. The camera "follows" you digitally without a camera operator.

The AI Video Workflow in 5 Steps

AI is no magic button — it's a toolbox. Here's the proven workflow pros use to integrate AI into production without losing creative control.

Idea & Script
AI brainstorming, outline generation and first script drafts
Pre-Production
Thumbnail generation, storyboard suggestions, location research
Edit & Cut
Auto-cut, transcription, chapter markers, color matching
Audio & GFX
Noise removal, auto-mix, music recommendations, title animation
Export & Publish
SEO optimization, auto-thumbnail, description generator, upload
The AI Rule: AI starts at 80% and you refine the last 20%. Never the other way around. AI delivers the rough structure — you turn it into a home.

AI Tools Overview

The landscape of AI video tools grows daily. Here are the most important categories with concrete tool recommendations for every budget.

Transcription & Subtitles
Whisper (OpenAI, free), Descript, Otter.ai. Convert speech to text — for subtitles, show notes and searchable video content.
Image Generation
Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion. Generate thumbnails, backgrounds and visual elements — custom-made for your video.
AI Edit & Auto-Cut
OpusClip, Runway ML, Descript. Automatic highlights, silence removal, scene detection and rough-cut generation.
Audio Enhancement
Adobe Podcast, Auphonic, Krisp. AI noise suppression, auto-leveling, voice enhancement — in real-time or post.
Stabilization & Reframe
DaVinci Resolve (Stabilizer), Runway ML, Topaz Video AI. Smooth shots from shaky footage, auto-reframe for different formats.
Text & SEO
ChatGPT, Claude, TubeBuddy. Generate video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnail ideas and community post copy.

Manual vs. AI-Assisted

When is AI worth it — and when is manual work indispensable? The key lies in conscious use, not blind trust.

Better Manual
Creative decisions, storytelling structure, color grading with vision, audio mixing for emotional scenes, fine-cut and timing adjustments.
Better with AI
Transcription, noise removal, auto-cut of pauses, thumbnail generation, format conversion, chapter markers, SEO copy, voice translation.
Perfect Combined
AI generates the rough cut, you refine. AI suggests thumbnails, you select and optimize. AI transcribes, you correct and format.
Pro Tip: AI makes mistakes. It hallucinates, misses nuances and sometimes produces nonsense. Always check the result — especially facts, names and sensitive content.

AI Workflows in Practice

Three concrete workflows you can implement immediately — regardless of your experience level.

YouTube Tutorial Workflow

  • ChatGPT: Generate outline and script structure (5 min.)
  • Recording: Capture video + separate audio
  • Descript/Whisper: Transcription for subtitles (2 min.)
  • OpusClip: Silence removal and rough cut (10 min.)
  • DaVinci Resolve: Fine cut, color, motion graphics (30 min.)
  • Midjourney/DALL-E: Generate thumbnail (5 min.)
  • ChatGPT: Title, description, tags, timestamp chapters (3 min.)

Documentary Workflow

  • ChatGPT: Research questions and interview guide (10 min.)
  • Adobe Podcast/Krisp: Audio cleanup for all interviews (15 min.)
  • Whisper: Transcription for archive and subtitles (5 min.)
  • DaVinci Resolve: Stabilization for handheld shots (10 min.)
  • Runway ML: B-roll generation for missing scenes (20 min.)
  • ChatGPT: Fact-check and source list (10 min.)

Social Short Workflow

  • OpusClip: Break long-form video into shorts (5 min.)
  • Auto-Reframe: Convert 16:9 to 9:16 (2 min.)
  • Caption Generator: Animated subtitles (3 min.)
  • Topaz Video AI: Upscale and enhancement (automatic)
  • ChatGPT: Hook copy and caption ideas (5 min.)
  • Midjourney: Thumb-stopping thumbnail (3 min.)

Dare to Practice

You only learn AI tools by trying them. Start with a simple workflow and expand it step by step.

Level 1 — First AI Transcription
Upload a 5-minute video to Whisper Online or Descript. Export the transcription as SRT subtitles and embed them in your video. Time: 15 min.
Level 2 — AI Rough Cut
Use OpusClip or Descript to create a 3-minute highlight cut from a 20-minute video. Refine the AI suggestion manually. Time: 30 min.
Level 3 — Complete AI Workflow
Create a complete YouTube video with AI support: script outline, transcription, auto-cut, thumbnail and SEO copy. Time: 90 min.
