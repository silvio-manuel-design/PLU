Highscore Game

Build a click game with counter and high score. The record survives reloading. Time: about 90 min.

Your task

A square target jumps randomly around the play field. Every hit scores one point. After 20 seconds the round ends. The high score goes to localStorage and survives reloading.

← Back to the projects

Requirements

Only tick a line when your code really does it.

Step by step

  1. Create project-1-highscore.html. Build play field, target element and counter in HTML and CSS.

  2. Write a click listener on the target. Raise the counter and move the target with random numbers.

  3. Start a 20-second clock with setInterval. Then disable the target and show the result.
  4. Read the high score from localStorage on load. After the round, store the better value.
  5. Build the “Play again” button. It resets counter and target.
  6. Test the game at 360 px. The play field must not scroll.
  7. Ask AI for one improvement. Implement exactly one.
  8. Check off every requirement above.
