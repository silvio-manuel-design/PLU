Lab 08 — Your Media Library

Objects describe the world. You build a media library with three entries. Time: about 30 min.

Your task

You create three objects — a film, a book and a song. Each has a title, a year and a rating. Your page lists all three and marks the best rating.

Step by step

  1. Build the three objects — each with the three properties.
  2. Create the array library.
  3. Write the describe() method on one object.
  4. Loop through the array: title (year) — rating of 5.
  5. Find the maximum with Math.max and mark the title.
