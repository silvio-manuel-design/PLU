Allowed tools

You only use what you learned in chapters 01–08. Everything else is forbidden.

Allowed

  • Variables: const and let
  • Text and numbers (strings, numbers)
  • Input: prompt()
  • Building text: template literals (backticks ` and ${`{`}…})
  • Branches: if, else
  • Loops: for
  • Arrays
  • Objects: properties, methods, this, dot and bracket notation
  • Functions: return, parameters
  • Output: console.log, innerHTML, getElementById
  • HTML/CSS: basic structure, embedded with defer

Not allowed

  • Events and addEventListener — they come in chapters 09 and 10
  • Forms — they come in chapter 11

If the AI sends you code with words from the “Not allowed” list — do not use it. Rather ask the AI: “Explain to me …”. You build it yourself.

Design guidelines

Your page is dark. Your code is in the title. The top work stands out clearly.

Guidelines

  • Dark background, like the chapter pages
  • Your code in the page title, clearly visible
  • The three entries are listed one below the other, easy to read
  • The top entry is marked with a colour or a label

Your favourite work gets your number as its rating. If it is greater than 5, take 5.

Requirements

Tick every line only when your code really does it. At the end, print the list as a PDF.

Step by step

Six steps. Plan about 20 minutes. After each step, reload your page.

  1. 1

    Create the folder

    Create the folder uebung-08/. Inside it index.html, css/ and js/. Create css/style.css and js/main.js and link them.

  2. 2

    Skeleton and design

    Heading h1 with your code. Dark background in style.css. An element with id="liste" for the output.

  3. 3

    Three objects

    Create film, book and song as objects — each with title, year and rating. Your favourite gets your number as its rating (maximum 5).

  4. 4

    Array and loop

    Put the three objects into the array library. A for loop writes each entry as a sentence into #liste.

  5. 5

    Top and method

    Find the best rating with Math.max and mark the entry. Write the describe() method on one object.

  6. 6

    Test and tick

    Reload the page: three sentences, one Top label, no errors in the console. Then tick the requirements.
