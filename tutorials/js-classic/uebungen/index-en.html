Exercises

Every chapter has an exercise. You solve it at home — with AI at your side. Your teacher announces the exercise and the deadline.

How it works every week

  1. Your teacher announces the exercise. Usually one per week.
  2. Open the lab and read the task. The requirements are at the bottom.
  3. Solve the task with AI. Use the starter prompt inside the lab.
  4. Check the code. Answer the three check questions honestly.
  5. Upload the file to your GitHub repository. Guide: GitHub & Abgabe.
  6. Tick off the exercise here. Then you know what you have done.
