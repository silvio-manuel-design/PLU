FINAL PROJECT

Your Final Project

One project, several weeks, your topic. Pick from the catalogue or submit your own idea. At the end, you present your work to the class.

Mandatory — with a grade boost

The final project is the mandatory assignment at the end of the course. Unlike the projects — which are voluntary — this one means: you work on your own product for several weeks and present it. Your teacher grades it together with your labs, tests and participation checks.

If tests or technical interviews went badly, an additional final project can improve your grade. Talk to your teacher.

How your final project works

  1. 1

    Pick your project.

    Catalogue or your own proposal — your teacher approves it.

  2. 2

    Plan on paper.

    Sketch areas, data and flows before you type.

  3. 3

    Build in steps.

    The stepper on your project page guides you.

  4. 4

    Use AI as your sparring partner.

    It explains and checks — you decide.

  5. 5

    Present your work.

    You show it to the class and answer questions.

Presentation

The presentation — Your project becomes your technical interview: you present your work to the class and answer your teacher's questions. Typical questions: Why did you choose this structure? How do you store your data? What was the biggest hurdle? Prepare your project website so it shows your answers — screenshots and reflection belong there.
