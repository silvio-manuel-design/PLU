The three rules

1

Understand it yourself first, then ask the AI

If you know nothing, you can't judge the answer. Read first, ask second.

2

Check every AI answer

In the course, in the playground or on MDN. Confidently wrong is the most common error type.

3

Rewrite it in your own words

Rewriting AI answers IS the learning. If you copy, you learn nothing.

Good prompts, bad prompts

Bad: „do the task"

No goal, no context — the answer will be arbitrary.

Good: „Show me an example of a for loop with comments, then explain line 3"

Bad: „explain code"

Which code? Which level? The AI has to guess.

Good: „Explain this function line by line, as if I were a beginner: [paste code]"

Bad: „it doesn't work"

Without error message and code, nobody can help.

Good: „This code throws ‚x is not defined' — line 4. What's the cause? [paste code + error]"

How to spot AI mistakes

Warning signs: overly general answers, outdated syntax, invented methods, missing brackets. Antidote: test in the playground and cross-check on MDN — two minutes that clear up every self-doubt.

AI & school

Honesty in homework: the reflections in this course have cheat protection — platitudes don't count. AI as a learning partner is allowed (ask, check, have things explained) — as a replacement it is not (submitting without understanding). Your teacher's rule always applies.
