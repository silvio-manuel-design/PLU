PANTAM LEARNING UNIVERSE

About the course

JavaScript V2 is the evolution of JavaScript Classic — the Pantam Learning Universe course that takes you from your first click to your own web app. Step by step, at your own pace.

The PLU platform at pantam.at (external link, opens in a new window)

FIRST, LET'S UNDERSTAND

The course follows a simple arc: 12 chapters teach you the concepts, 12 labs let you practise them on small real tasks, 3 projects combine everything — and in the capstone you present your own work to the class.

You only need two things: a browser and curiosity. Everything else — tools, explanations, exercises — is right here.
