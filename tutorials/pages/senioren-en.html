Seniors

For Seniors

Understand technology step by step

Getting Started

Start without fear

You have been learning your whole life – why stop now? AI is not witchcraft, but a tool that can make your everyday life easier.

The best time to learn something new was 20 years ago. The second best time is now. AI is like a good neighbour: polite, helpful and patient.

You are not too old – the technology is young enough

AI tools are more user-friendly today than ever before. You don't need a degree in computer science, to benefit from technology. With simple words and patience, PLU guides you step by step into the digital world.

Benefits

What does this do for me?

Communication

Write emails, letters or messages effortlessly – even in foreign languages. AI helps you, formulates politely and adjusts the tone to your needs.

Learn more

Everyday Life

Organise appointments, reminders and shopping lists. Have recipes explained to you or questions about health topics answered – understandable and without technical jargon.

Learn more

Hobbies

Discover new creative possibilities: edit old photos, write stories or get inspired while gardening, crafting or cooking.

Learn more

Independence

Stay independent: AI helps with authorities, forms and online services. You keep control of your life – digital and analog.

Learn more
Identity

Your identity remains

AI does not replace your experience – it complements it.

You remain the boss. AI is your assistant, not your supervisor. Your life experience, your values and your decisions matter – today more than ever.

Practice

Practical Examples

Writing letters

Tell the AI what you want to communicate – it will formulate it politely and understandably. Perfect for official correspondence or loving messages to your grandchildren.

Enhancing old photos

Have blurry or damaged photos restored. AI can restore colours and bring memories to life – all without expensive software.

Overcoming language barriers

Travel or communicate in other languages. AI doesn't just translate words, it explains cultural differences – for real understanding.

Basics

What is AI?

Like a calculator

Imagine AI as a very intelligent calculator. You can use it to calculate, write, plan and organise – just much more versatile.

Like a conversation

You speak or write with AI as you would with a person. It understands your questions and gives answers – in English, simple and patient.
