Your Journey

Your Journey Begins Here

Are you between 14 and 25 years old and want to understand the digital world? At PLU you don't just learn how technology works – you learn how to use it for your goals.

In a world that is changing rapidly, curiosity and digital competence are your greatest advantages. We accompany you on your way.

Why PLU?

We combine technical knowledge with creative thinking. You learn web development, how to use AI as a tool, and build a portfolio that impresses in school, university, and career.
