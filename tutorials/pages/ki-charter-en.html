Our Values

AI Charter

Four Pillars for Responsible AI Use

Guiding Principle

Our Promise

These four principles form the foundation of our Learning Universe. They guide us in our interaction with AI and define how we use technology responsibly for education and development.

AI is a mirror – it reveals how you think and who you are. Artificial intelligence supports us; it does not replace us. We use AI to think better, not to delegate thinking.

A Shared Compass

The AI Charter is not a rigid set of rules, but a shared compass. It helps us seize the opportunities of AI without losing sight of human responsibility.

The Four Pillars

Our Principles

Critical Thinking

AI generates suggestions – we verify, question, and decide.

The final decision always lies with humans. AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgement.

In Practice

  • Check Sources: AI can hallucinate. Always verify facts with trustworthy sources before using them.
  • Question: Critically question assumptions in AI outputs. Why does the AI suggest this? What data basis does it have?
  • Own Judgement: Trust your expertise and intuition. You know the context better than any AI.
  • Responsibility: The final decision always lies with you – never the AI. You bear the consequences.

Why This Matters

AI systems are trained on data from the internet – with all its errors, prejudices, and biases. They can generate convincingly sounding but false information ("hallucinations"). Critical thinking is therefore not optional, but essential.

In our courses, you learn to formulate prompts to get better results – but also how to systematically evaluate those results.

Human Responsibility

Technology serves humanity. Not the other way around.

We retain control over our tools. The ethics of action lie with humans.

Your Responsibility

  • Tool Selection: You decide which AI tools you use and how. You are not forced to use every technology.
  • Usage: You decide how you use and adapt AI outputs. Copy-paste is rarely the best solution.
  • Final Result: You bear full responsibility for your work – even if AI helped.
  • Ethical Boundaries: You determine the ethical boundaries of your actions. What is allowed with AI, what is not?

Societal Dimension

AI influences decisions in job applications, credit lending, and law enforcement. Those who use AI share responsibility for its impact on other people.

We promote awareness of this responsibility – through transparent discussion and reflection in our community.

Practical Application

Theory without practice is empty. Practice without theory is blind.

We learn by doing. Every concept is immediately implemented and tested in concrete projects.

Our Approach

  • Immediate Application: No dry theory – every lesson ends with a practical exercise or a project.
  • Iterative Learning: Try things out, make mistakes, improve. That is the fastest way to mastery.
  • Relevance: We show you how AI helps in your specific everyday work – not in abstract scenarios.
  • Exchange: Learn from other participants. Share your results and receive constructive feedback.

From Beginner to Expert

Our learning paths are designed so that after each module you hold a concrete result in your hands – a finished prompt, an analysed document, an automated task.

This strengthens not only your understanding but also your confidence in using AI.

Data Sovereignty

Your data belongs to you. Period.

We respect your privacy. No tracking, no sharing, no compromises.

Our Commitments

  • No Tracking: We do not track what you learn on our platform. No analytics tools that record your behaviour.
  • Data Protection: Your personal data is never passed on to third parties. Not sold, not shared.
  • Transparency: You always know what data we store and why. Full control over your account.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request the deletion of all your data at any time – without justification, without waiting time.

Open Source & Independence

Our entire platform is open source. That means: you can check what happens under the hood. No hidden trackers, no surprising data transfers.

We are funded by donations, not by your data. That makes us independent and you secure.
