Commitment

Learner Pledge

The commitment for responsible learning

A Promise

A Promise to Yourself

AI changes everything. How we learn, work, create. But with great power comes great responsibility.

This Pledge is not an obligation – it is an invitation. A promise to use AI consciously and responsibly.

The decision is yours.

The 4 Pillars

My Commitment

Four principles that make the difference between blind consumption and conscious action.

1

I remain the pilot

I steer. The AI executes.

AI is my tool, not my replacement. I retain control over goals, decisions, and the final result.

"The AI is my co-pilot – I remain the captain."
2

I verify before I trust

Every output is a draft – until I have validated it.

AI hallucinates, makes mistakes, invents sources. I never trust blindly. I check facts, question logic, and validate.

"Trust, but verify – Trust is good, verification is better."
3

I keep my voice

The result should sound like me, not like a machine.

AI provides raw material. I give it my personal touch, my perspective, my authenticity.

"Would I sign this? Does it match who I am?"
4

I share responsibly

What I pass on shapes others. I act with care.

AI-generated content can spread errors, reinforce biases. I only share what I have verified.

"With great power comes great responsibility."
