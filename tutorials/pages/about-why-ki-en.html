AI Basics

Why AI?

AI as a tool. You're in control. An inclusive future.

The Reality

Understanding AI, staying human

Imagine: You complete in 30 minutes what used to take an entire day. You suddenly have time for the things that really matter – creative ideas, strategic decisions, conversations with people.

AI is no longer an experiment. It writes texts, generates images, analyses data, programmes alongside you. But here's the crucial point: AI doesn't think. It only reflects what we give it.

The difference? You decide. You're the pilot, not the passenger.

Mindset

The Operator

Imagine an aeroplane pilot. They utilise the best instrumentation in the world – but remain the decision-maker. They check, validate, and take responsibility.

1

Tool Thinking

You're in control. The AI executes.

The AI is your tool, not your boss. You set the direction, it handles the execution.

"I utilise AI to be faster – not to delegate."
2

Plan Before Prompt

Goal first, then input.

Those who prompt without a plan get planless results. Define what you want to achieve.

"What do I want to hold in my hands at the end? Who am I reaching?"
3

Test Before Trust

Check before you publish.

AI hallucinates, makes mistakes, exaggerates. Never trust blindly. Every output is a draft.

"Check facts, verify sources, test logic."
4

Your Voice

AI supports, but doesn't replace.

The result should sound like you, not like a machine. Adapt, refine, make it your own.

"Would I sign this? Does this fit me?"

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I be replaced?

No. AI replaces tasks, not people. Those who master AI become more valuable – because they can think strategically whilst others are stuck in routine.

Who's in control?

Always you. The AI is your tool, not your boss. Without your checking, every output is just a draft.

Is it safe?

Yes – with smart rules. Public AI tools for public content. Sensitive data? Enterprise solutions. You stay in control.

What does it cost?

At PLU: Nothing. Our basic courses are free. For advanced tools, there are free and paid options – you decide.

How long does learning take?

The basics in a few hours. With our Operator Mindset, you can become productive immediately. Mastery comes with practice – step by step.

Is it future-proof?

Absolutely. AI competence is the key to the working world of tomorrow. Those who start now shape the future instead of just watching.
