Why support?

Education is not a privilege

PANTAM, Art & Culture is a non-profit association for art, culture and education. We are funded exclusively by donations.

"The best time to learn something new was 20 years ago. The second best time is now. AI is like a good neighbour: Polite, helpful and patient."

Every donation counts. Whether 5 € or 50 € – you enable people of all ages to gain free access to high-quality AI education. No paywalls, no subscriptions, no advertising.

All our tutorials are open source and freely available. There are no financial burdens for users. That is our promise – and your support makes it possible.

100% of your donation goes directly into education. Server costs, new tutorials, better technology – transparent and comprehensible.
