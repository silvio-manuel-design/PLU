For Parents

Trust through transparency

Welcome

Dear Parents

Would you like to know what your child learns at PLU and how AI supports the learning process? Here you will find all the information – transparent and easy to understand.

"AI is not a replacement for teachers, but a tool like a calculator or an encyclopedia – only one that responds to your child individually and meets them where they currently are."

Trust matters to us

We understand that you care about your child's education. That is why we openly explain how we use AI, what data we store and how we securely document learning progress.

Learning Content

What does my child learn?

1. Understanding AI

Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence: What is AI? How does it work? What are its limits?

2. Using AI

Practical application: Effective prompting, critically questioning AI responses, productive collaboration.

3. Evaluating AI

Responsible use: Data protection, copyright, ethical principles, recognising limits.

Independent projects: From idea to implementation – with AI as a creative partner.

Assessment

Grades and Fairness

How we ensure that your child really learns

1

We use AI as a learning tool, not as a copying machine – real understanding comes first.

2

Every submission is checked for plausibility – we notice when something does not fit.

3

Oral exams and project presentations supplement written work.

4

Transparency in AI usage: Those who use AI document it honestly.

5

Focus on process over result: The learning journey matters more than the perfect answer.

Security

Security & Privacy

Data Protection

We store only the minimum. No sensitive data, no sharing with third parties.

Content Safety

All content is checked for age-appropriateness. No inappropriate topics.

Transparency

You can see at any time what your child is learning. All progress is traceable.

Control

You stay in control. Viewable at any time, adjustable at any time.

Participation

Your Participation

Observe

Take a look at the content. We have nothing to hide.

Dialogue

Feedback

Your feedback helps us. Tell us about your experiences.
