Letter to Your Landlord

With ChatGPT or Claude – free & no registration needed

1

Open the AI

Go to chatgpt.com or claude.ai in your browser. No download needed – it works directly in the browser.

2

Enter the prompt

Type into the text field:

"Formulate a polite letter to my landlord that the heating hasn't been working for three days. I am a retiree and the apartment is very cold. Please request a quick repair."
3

Review the draft

Read the suggestion. Is something missing? Simply type: "Add that I am over 70 and the cold affects my health." – The AI will adjust the letter immediately.

4

Use it

Click the copy icon and paste the text into your email program – or print it out.

Explain AI Simply

With Microsoft Copilot or ChatGPT – for conversations with grandchildren

1

Open the AI

Visit copilot.microsoft.com or chatgpt.com in your browser. Both are free to use.

2

Request the explanation

Type into the text field:

"Explain to a 10-year-old simply what Artificial Intelligence is. Use an example from everyday life that a child can understand."
3

Make it understandable

Too complicated? Type: "Make it even simpler, like a short story." Or: "Explain the technical terms you used."

4

Have the conversation

Tell your grandchild what you learned. Ask them: "What do you actually know about AI?" – This way you become a dialogue partner instead of a bystander.

Plan a Trip with AI

With ChatGPT or Google Gemini – tailored to your pace

1

Open the AI

Open chatgpt.com or gemini.google.com in your browser. Both services are free.

2

Formulate your wish

Type into the text field:

"Plan a 3-day trip to Salzburg for seniors. Comfortable pace, good restaurants, no long walks. I am interested in classical music and beautiful views."
3

Adjust the plan

Would you like to change something? Type: "Add a Mozart concert on Saturday evening" or "Recommend a quiet café with cake in the old town."

4

Save it

Copy the finished plan and save it in a notes app – or send it to yourself by email. This way you have everything at hand.
