Our Approach

Three Principles for Your Success

This tutorial was developed with a clear intention

01

Fear is normal

Everyone feels overwhelmed at the beginning. This is not a sign of weakness, but of intelligence – you are questioning something new.

AI has been deliberately portrayed as complex. Media loves the "magic". We show you the simple truth behind it.
