AI as a Creative Partner

The Palette Analogy

Creativity is no longer an exclusively human privilege – but the real art remains up to you

The Artist and Their Palette

Imagine a painter: they have a palette of colors, brushes, and a canvas. AI is like an extended palette – it offers endless shades you didn't have before. But deciding WHAT to paint and HOW it looks remains your job.

The three pillars of AI creativity:
  • Image Generation: From concept to finished image
  • Ideation: Brainstorming without end
  • Creative Projects: From storytelling to music concepts

Prompting for Images

Describe in detail: style, colors, mood, lighting – the more precise, the better.

"A cottage in an English garden, watercolor style, morning mist, soft light"
Aha!

A good image prompt works like a description for a painter: the more specific, the better. Try: "A red cottage in an English garden, watercolor style, morning mist, soft light"

Iterate & Refine

The first image is rarely perfect. Use feedback loops to optimize.

Change individual elements: "more contrast", "different perspective"
Aha!

The first image is like a first draft. AI understands your feedback: "more contrast", "different perspective", "less blue". Each adjustment brings you closer to your vision.

Styles & References

From oil painting to pixel art – AI masters countless artistic styles.

Mention artists or eras: "in the style of Monet", "Art Deco"
Aha!

You can ask AI for styles of famous artists: "in the style of Monet" or "Art Deco". But remember: AI doesn't copy, it interprets – your artistic judgment remains the decisive factor.

Ideation

Creative Techniques with AI

Brainstorming, mind mapping, and more – how AI expands your creativity

0 of 4 discovered
01

Mind Mapping

Have AI create chains of associations – from a core concept to endless branches.

Pro Tip

Start with a simple word like "garden". AI suggests branches: flowers, tools, seasons, memories. Each branch opens new doors. Try it: What comes to mind when you think of "kitchen"?

02

Combination

Connect things that seem incompatible. AI finds bridges between worlds.

Pro Tip

The secret of good ideas: Connect two things no one has put together yet. "A recipe + a travel story" = A culinary travel guide. What will you connect?

03

Reverse Thinking

Ask: "What would be the opposite?" – often the best ideas arise this way.

Pro Tip

Ask AI: "What would the opposite look like?" If you want a calm text, ask for an excited one. Then flip it. Opposites show us what we truly want.

04

Role Swap

Let AI think from different perspectives: child, expert, artist, critic.

Pro Tip

Let AI think from different perspectives: "As an 8-year-old, explain to me..." or "As an expert in...". Each perspective brings new facets. Your life experience helps you choose the best one.

Keep Your Own Voice

Where AI reaches its limits and why your imagination remains irreplaceable

0 of 3 discovered

Emotional Depth

AI can describe love, but it cannot feel. Authentic emotions come from you.

Aha!

AI describes love with words, but it has never loved. When you write a letter to your grandchildren, the warmth comes from you – AI is just the pen, not the heart.

Personal Experience

Your life story is unique. AI has no memories, no biography.

Aha!

Your childhood, your travels, your dreams – that's material only you have. AI can help write down your stories, but the stories themselves belong to you alone.

Artistic Vision

The decision WHAT to create always remains yours. AI is the tool, not the artist.

Aha!

The decision about WHAT you want to create is and remains yours. AI is the tool – like a brush or a camera. The artist is always you.
