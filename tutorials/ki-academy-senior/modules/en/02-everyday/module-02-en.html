Myth Busting

Facts vs. Fiction

Let's clear up the biggest misunderstandings. Click to uncover.

❌ Myth

"AI is too complicated for me"

Technology is only for young people and experts.

✓ Fact

AI is as simple as a TV remote. You press a button — AI does the rest. No programming needed.

❌ Myth

"AI spies on me"

AI stores everything I do and sells my data.

✓ Fact

You decide what AI learns about you. Like a good neighbor: helpful, but not intrusive. You can always say 'no'.

❌ Myth

"AI does everything automatically"

AI takes control and I can no longer decide anything.

✓ Fact

AI makes suggestions, you make decisions. Like GPS navigation: you choose whether to follow the route or take your own path.

❌ Myth

"AI is only for young people"

Older people can't do anything with AI.

✓ Fact

The best AI users are the curious — regardless of age. Experience and wisdom help you use AI meaningfully.

"Wisdom is not knowing everything - but knowing where and how to find knowledge."

— Samuel Johnson
