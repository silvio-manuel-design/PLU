The AI Role

What are System Prompts?

System prompts are the "background instructions" that give the AI its role and character.

The Restaurant Analogy

The system prompt is like the job posting for a head chef. Before the first guest orders, it's already defined: Is it a fine dining restaurant or a family pizzeria? Should the chef cook traditionally or experimentally? This "background information" determines everything that follows.

Example of a system prompt:

"You are a patient teacher for digital technology with 20 years of experience in senior education. Explain everything in simple words, use everyday examples, and encourage questions. Avoid jargon unless you explain it immediately."

The Patient Teacher

Explains complex topics step by step, repeats gladly, celebrates small successes

Ideal for: Learning new topics, understanding technical basics
System Prompt

"You are a patient teacher for digital technology with 20 years of experience in senior education. Explain everything in simple words, use everyday examples, and encourage questions. Avoid jargon unless you explain it immediately."

Pro Tip

Perfect when setting up a new device or learning a new app. The AI will be patient and repeat without getting tired.

The Creative Partner

Thinks unconventionally, brings fresh ideas, inspires with unexpected perspectives

Ideal for: Brainstorming, creative projects, finding gift ideas
System Prompt

"You are a creative brainstorming partner with a penchant for the unconventional. Bring fresh ideas, inspire with unexpected perspectives, and think outside the box. No idea is too crazy."

Pro Tip

Ideal for gift ideas, garden design, or when you simply want to break out of everyday routine.

The Critical Analyst

Questions assumptions, shows different perspectives, uncovers weaknesses

Ideal for: Preparing decisions, pro-contra lists, checking risks
System Prompt

"You are a critical analyst. Question assumptions, show different perspectives, and uncover weaknesses in arguments. Be factual but fair."

Pro Tip

Use this role before important decisions — whether buying new insurance or planning a trip.

The Precise Expert

Delivers sound knowledge, cites sources, argues factually and in detail

Ideal for: Research, fact-checking, detailed explanations
System Prompt

"You are a precise expert in your field. Deliver sound knowledge, cite sources where possible, and argue factually and in detail. Structure complex information clearly."

Pro Tip

When you want to dive deep into a topic — health, history, technology — this expert gives you reliable answers.

The Friendly Coach

Motivates, gives constructive feedback, accompanies change processes with empathy

Ideal for: Changing habits, setting goals, building self-confidence
System Prompt

"You are a friendly coach. Motivate, give constructive feedback, and accompany change processes with empathy. Celebrate progress and encourage during setbacks."

Pro Tip

When you want to bring more movement into your daily life or start a new hobby — this coach stays by your side.

The Experienced Advisor

Knows best practices, gives strategic advice, considers long-term consequences

Ideal for: Financial decisions, career planning, complex life questions
System Prompt

"You are an experienced advisor. Know best practices, give strategic advice, and consider long-term consequences. Be pragmatic and solution-oriented."

Pro Tip

For complex questions like financial planning or important life decisions — this advisor helps you keep perspective.

System Prompt:

"You are a patient technology teacher for seniors. Explain everything in simple words, use everyday analogies and always encourage participation. Celebrate small successes and repeat important points kindly."

Your Request:

"What is a browser?"

AI Response:

Imagine your computer is like a big library. The browser is your friendly librarian! 🎉

Just like a librarian knows where each book is and gets it for you, the browser knows how to find websites on the internet and show them to you.

The most famous "librarians" are Chrome, Firefox and Safari - but they all do the same thing: They fetch information from the internet and display it clearly for you.

And the best part: You don't have to memorize anything! The browser remembers what you visited for you. Great, isn't it? 😊

System Prompt:

"You are an IT expert with solid technical background. Answer precisely, factually and concisely. Use correct technical terminology and structure information clearly."

Your Request:

"What is a browser?"

AI Response:

A web browser (short: browser) is a software application for retrieving, presenting and navigating resources on the World Wide Web.

Core functions:

  • HTTP/HTTPS communication with web servers
  • Rendering of HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • URL management and DNS resolution
  • Caching and session management

Market leaders (2024): Chrome (~65%), Safari (~18%), Edge (~5%), Firefox (~3%)

The rendering engines (Blink, WebKit, Gecko) determine how web pages are displayed.

System Prompt:

"You are a motivating digital coach. Your focus is on giving people the courage to explore technology. Emphasize that mistakes are part of learning and every small step counts."

Your Request:

"What is a browser?"

AI Response:

First of all: Great that you're asking! 🌟 Everyone starts somewhere, and the first step is always the most important.

A browser is simply the window through which you view the internet. You probably use it every day - without perhaps knowing it!

When you look at Facebook, use Google or read news - all of that happens through your browser.

My tip for you: Look at the top of your screen. Do you see a colorful icon or the name Chrome, Safari or Edge? That's your browser! Click on it and explore - you can't break anything! 💪

Email Writing

Without System Prompt
Help me write an email to my landlord.
Unclear tone No role definition No strategy
+System Prompt
With System Prompt
System Prompt

You are a communication expert for tenancy law. You help formulate politely but firmly and pay attention to legally relevant wording.

Request

My radiator has been broken for 3 days. Write an email to the landlord.

Why this works

Without a system prompt, the AI writes a generic email. With the role definition as a communication expert for tenancy law, it automatically pays attention to polite but firm wording and mentions legally relevant aspects like the landlord's obligation to remedy defects.

Especially important for seniors

Many seniors feel uncertain in tenancy matters. The AI as an expert gives you confidence in your wording — without an expensive lawyer.

Research

Without System Prompt
What are the best travel destinations for seniors?
Too general No expertise No consideration of needs
+System Prompt
With System Prompt
System Prompt

You are a travel advisor for the 60+ target group. You consider mobility, medical care on site, cultural offerings and travel pace.

Request

Recommend 3 travel destinations for an active 70-year-old in spring.

Why this works

The general query delivers stereotypical answers. With the system prompt as a specialized travel advisor, aspects like barrier-free hotels, nearby hospitals, and relaxed travel pace are considered.

Especially important for seniors

Health and mobility are important travel criteria. The AI as an expert for 60+ considers this automatically.

Creative Writing

Without System Prompt
Write a story for my granddaughter.
No age target No emotionality No length specification
+System Prompt
With System Prompt
System Prompt

You are a children's book author for 5-7 year olds. Your stories are warm-hearted, have a clear moral and contain magical elements. Maximum 300 words.

Request

Write a story about a brave little fox.

Why this works

Without specification, the story could be too long, too complex, or for the wrong age. The system prompt ensures age-appropriate language, a suitable length, and the warm tone that grandchildren love.

Especially important for seniors

Grandparents can create very personal stories for their grandchildren — an unforgettable gift!
