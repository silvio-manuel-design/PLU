The Foundation

What is a Prompt?

A prompt is your instruction to the AI - comparable to an order in a restaurant

The Restaurant Analogy

Imagine you're ordering at a restaurant. The more precisely you describe what you want - "A juicy steak, medium, with herb butter and seasonal vegetables" - the better your dish will be. The same principle applies to AI.

Example of a good prompt:

"Explain to me what a prompt is, as if I were a 10-year-old. Use a simple example from everyday life."

The 5 Building Blocks

The perfect prompt consists of:

Each building block fulfills a specific function. Click on the cards for examples.

0 of 5 blocks discovered
1

Context

The framework of your request

Aha!

Context is like the introduction to a conversation. When you want someone to help you, you first tell them who you are and what you need. The more personal the context, the better the response. Try it: "I am 70, interested in gardening and would like to..."

"I am 68 years old, retired, and want to use AI for my hobby photography..."
2

Role

Who should the AI play?

Aha!

The role is the most important trick! Tell the AI who it should play: "You are a patient teacher", "You are a travel expert for seniors", "You are my grandchild explaining technology to me". Each role brings a different perspective.

"You are a patient photography teacher with experience in senior education..."
3

Task

What should be achieved?

Aha!

Be specific! "Explain AI to me" is too vague. "Explain to me in 3 sentences how AI sorts my photos" is precise. Imagine you are giving instructions to a craftsman – the more precise, the better the result.

"Explain to me step by step how I can improve my landscape photos with AI..."
4

Format

How should the answer look?

Aha!

The format is your wish list! Would you like a numbered list? A table? A story? Simple language without technical terms? Tell the AI – it can deliver almost any format.

"Use simple language, numbered steps, and concrete examples from everyday life."
5

Constraints

What should be avoided?

Aha!

Limits are freedom! "Maximum 200 words", "Don't recommend expensive equipment", "Only Austrian examples". The more boundaries you set, the more focused the response becomes. That is not meant unkindly – that is precision.

"Avoid technical terms without explanation and stay under 300 words."

Wonderful! You have discovered all 5 building blocks!

Each building block is a key to better AI responses. Combine them for the perfect dialog.

Before / After

How to Improve Your Prompts

Concrete examples from practice with the 5 building blocks

Photography Tip

Weak
How do I take better photos?
Too general No role No format
+5 Blocks
Strong
Context

I am a beginner in landscape photography.

Role

You are an experienced photographer.

Task

Give me 5 concrete tips for better shots at sunrise.

Format

Use numbered lists with example photos.

Constraints

Avoid expensive special equipment.

Writing an Email

Weak
Write an email to the landlord.
No context No details
+5 Blocks
Strong
Context

I am a tenant in an apartment with a defective radiator.

Role

You are a polite but assertive communication expert.

Task

Write a polite complaint email to the landlord.

Format

Maximum 150 words, professional tone, with request for quick repair.

Constraints

No legal threats.

Health Tip

Weak
What helps against back pain?
Too unspecific No target group
+5 Blocks
Strong
Context

I am 70 years old, have mild back pain when gardening, and don't want to take medication.

Role

You are a physiotherapist with senior experience.

Task

Recommend 3 gentle exercises for the lower back.

Format

Simple instructions with image descriptions.

Constraints

No strenuous exercises, always include note: Consult doctor for stronger pain.

"The clearer your request, the better the response."
