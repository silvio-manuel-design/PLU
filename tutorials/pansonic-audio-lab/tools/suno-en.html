Prompting Guide

How to Write Good Prompts

A good Suno prompt is precise and structured. The more accurately you describe what you want, the better the result.

🎯 The Prompt Structure

The ideal structure for Suno prompts:

[Style] � Genre, subgenre, musical direction
[Mood] � Emotion, atmosphere, energy level
[Instruments] � Main instruments, special features
[Tempo/BPM] � Speed (optional)

Example Prompts

Electronic Dance Music

"Upbeat electronic dance music, energetic, synthesizer, 128 BPM"

Lo-Fi Hip Hop

"Lo-fi hip hop, relaxing, piano and rain sounds, study beats, chill atmosphere"

Epic Orchestral

"Epic orchestral trailer music, dramatic, strings and brass, cinematic, powerful"

Acoustic Pop

"Acoustic pop ballad, emotional, acoustic guitar and piano, heartfelt vocals, mid-tempo"

The Tags System

Suno understands certain tags particularly well. Use these tags for more precise results:

pop rock electronic hip-hop jazz classical indie upbeat melancholic energetic relaxing dramatic dreamy

❌ To Avoid

  • Too vague descriptions like "good music"
  • Contradictory styles ("aggressive jazz ballad")
  • Copying artist names (copyright!)
  • Prompts over 200 characters

Extend a Song

Extension only works with Pro subscription:

  1. Click on the song you want to extend
  2. Select "Extend" from the menu
  3. Describe how the song should continue (or use the same prompt)
  4. Suno seamlessly adds more sections

Make a Complete Song from MP3

Have a short clip you want to extend?

  1. Upload your MP3 to Suno
  2. Describe the desired style for the extension
  3. Suno analyzes the clip and generates matching continuations
  4. Select the best variation and combine it with the original

AI Ethics & Rights

Copyright with AI Music

The legal situation with AI-generated music is complex and constantly evolving:

  • Free version: Songs are intended for personal use
  • Pro/Premier: Commercial use is allowed (check current T&Cs)
  • You own the rights to your prompts, not necessarily to the AI output
  • Different regulations apply in different countries

Commercial Use

With a Pro or Premier subscription, you may use Suno songs commercially:

  • Monetize YouTube videos
  • Use podcast music
  • Use in your own projects
  • Publish on streaming platforms (with restrictions)

⚠️ Important

Read the current Terms of Service from Suno carefully. Rights can change. For commercial projects, legal advice is recommended.

Responsible Use

  • Respect the work of human musicians
  • Be transparent about AI-generated content
  • Use Suno as a tool, not a replacement for creativity
  • Don't clone real artists or their voices
  • Watch for sensitive content in lyrics
