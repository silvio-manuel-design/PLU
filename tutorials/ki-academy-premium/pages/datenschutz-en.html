Collection of Personal Data

We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This website was designed with the goal of collecting as little personal data as possible.

What we store

  • Local settings: Theme preferences (light/dark) are stored exclusively in your browser (LocalStorage)
  • Server logs: When you visit, technically necessary access data (IP address, timestamp, page accessed) is stored for a maximum of 7 days
  • Cookies: No tracking or analytics cookies are used

What we do NOT store

  • No personal data without your explicit consent
  • No data to third parties for analysis or advertising
  • No location data or device information
  • No data from minors without parental consent

Purpose of Data Processing

The processing of your data is carried out exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Provision of the website: Technically necessary for operation
  • User-friendliness: Storage of theme settings
  • Security: Detection and defense against attacks
  • Troubleshooting: Identification of technical problems

Data Minimization

Our principle is data minimization. We only collect the data that is absolutely necessary for the operation of the website and store it only as long as necessary.

Cookies and Tracking

This website uses no tracking cookies and does not set cookies for advertising purposes. The only form of local data storage is LocalStorage for theme settings.

What is LocalStorage?

LocalStorage is a browser feature that allows small amounts of data to be stored locally on your device. This data is:

  • Stored only on your device (not on our servers)
  • Not shared with third parties
  • Not used for tracking
  • Can be deleted by you at any time (browser settings)

Your Rights (GDPR)

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

Access

You have the right to obtain information about the personal data we store about you at any time.

Rectification

You can request the correction of inaccurate data or the completion of incomplete data.

Erasure

You have the right to have your data deleted ("right to be forgotten"), provided there are no statutory retention obligations.

Restriction

Under certain circumstances, you can request the restriction of the processing of your data.

Data Portability

You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format and to transfer it to another provider.

Objection

You can object to the processing of your data at any time, insofar as it is based on legitimate interests.
