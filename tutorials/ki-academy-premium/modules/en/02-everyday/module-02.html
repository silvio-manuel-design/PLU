The Mirror Principle

AI Is Your Mirror

The clearer your input, the better the answer. Good prompts are the key.

Weak Input

"Write something about marketing"

Generic text without focus

vs

Strong Input

"Create a social media post for an organic café in Vienna – humorous, target group 25-40"

Precise, target-group-oriented content

It's not the AI that needs to adapt – you shape the result through your input. Concrete prompts lead to precise answers.

Practical Examples

AI in Everyday Life: Three Concrete Applications

From email to research – save time and energy.

Emails & Communication

Formulate difficult messages, translate texts professionally, or summarize long emails.

Example: "Formulate a polite rejection to a customer asking for a discount. Professional, but friendly."

Research & Learning

Explain complex topics simply, research facts, or create customized learning plans.

Example: "Explain the basics of blockchain technology to me like I'm an absolute beginner in 5 points."

Creative Writing

Brainstorm ideas, create content calendars, or optimize existing texts for SEO.

Example: "Create 10 ideas for Instagram posts for a yoga studio – mindful, authentic, without esotericism."

"The best AI is the one you use daily – not the one that gets the most hype."

Getting Started

Your First Steps with AI

Three simple principles for a successful start.

01

Be Specific

The more specific your request, the more precise the answer. Name context, target audience, and desired tone.

"Explain this to me like I'm 10 years old"
02

Iterate

Perfection rarely comes on the first try. Request adjustments, ask follow-up questions, refine step by step.

"Make it shorter and more professional"
03

Experiment

Test different formulations. Every AI reacts differently – find your own style.

"List the pros and cons"
