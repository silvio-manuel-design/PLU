Common Pitfalls When Combining

        

        

          

            

              1
              

                Overloaded Prompts

                Don't pack ALL techniques into EVERY prompt. Choose selectively!
              

            

            

              2
              

                Contradictory Instructions

                CoT "step by step" but also "short answer" → Collision!
              

            

            

              3
              

                Too Many Examples + CoT

                Watch the context limit! Better 2 good examples than 5 mediocre ones.
              

            

            

              4
              

                Forgetting Complexity

                For simple tasks, a simple prompt is enough. Don't over-engineer!
