The Roleplay Experiment

Imagine asking the same person about the same topic three times – but in three different roles:

  • As a strict professor you get technical jargon
  • As a patient teacher you get simple explanations
  • As a hyped YouTuber you get "Guys, this is insane! 🔥"

The same applies to AI! Through roles (personas) you can control how the AI responds – not just what it says. This is your secret weapon for creative, precise, or entertaining answers!

👤 What are Personas?

The Definition

A persona is a fictional role you give the AI. It determines the tone, style, and perspective of the response.

🎬

Think of the AI as an actor. You are the director saying: "In this scene, you're a skeptical scientist." Suddenly the AI questions everything and demands evidence!

🔄 The Experiment: One Topic, Three Roles

Topic: Why is the sky blue?
🧑‍🔬

The Physics Professor

"You are a physics professor. Explain technically precise."

The blue color of the sky results from Rayleigh scattering of electromagnetic radiation by molecules in Earth's atmosphere. Shorter wavelengths (blue light, approx. 450-495 nm) are scattered more strongly than longer...

👵

The Loving Grandma

"You are a loving grandma. Explain simply and warmly."

Imagine, sunlight is like a rainbow with many colors. The blue color is very light and gets "caught" by the sky. That's why we see blue everywhere when we look up!

🎤

The Hyped YouTuber

"You are a hyped YouTuber. Be enthusiastic and emotional."

GUYS! Do you know why the sky is BLUE?! 🤯 This is absolutely WILD! The light makes such a CRAZY trip through the air and the blue gets SCATTERED everywhere! Mind = blown! 🔥🔥🔥

🔥 Pro Level: Role + Context Combined

The real magic happens when you combine role + context:

LEVEL 1

Role Only

"You are a historian. Explain World War II."
OK, but generic
LEVEL 2

Role + Target Audience

"You are a historian. Explain World War II to a 14-year-old."
Better, adapted!
LEVEL 3

Role + Target Audience + Purpose + Format

"You are a historian who makes complex topics exciting. I am a student (14) and need to prepare a presentation. Explain the main causes of WWII as an exciting story with 3 chapters, maximum 5 minutes reading time."
PERFECT! 🎯
🌍 LANGUAGE TIP

Roles in both languages!

Some roles sound more authentic in one language:

🇩🇪 Deutsch
"Du bist ein strenger deutscher Professor mit Doktortitel..."

The "German professor" is a clichéd expectation

VS
🇺🇸 English
"You are a hype California tech bro..."

The "tech bro" persona is a typically English internet phenomenon

💡

Pro tip: Use cultural clichés! "A British butler" works better in English, "a strict German teacher" in German. The AI knows these cultural codes!
