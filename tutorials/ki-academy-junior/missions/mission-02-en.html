Why the first prompt matters

Imagine entering a restaurant. Your first sentence to the waiter determines how the entire evening goes. Do you just say "Food" – or "I'd like a table for two by the window, and I'm allergic to nuts"?

The same applies to AI. A good first prompt is like a perfect handshake – it sets the framework for everything that follows.

📋 The Perfect Greeting Formula

👤 WHO Introduce yourself
+
🎯 WHAT Your goal
+
📊 HOW The format
=
✨ PERFECT PROMPT
❌ BAD
"Write something about climate"

Too vague – AI doesn't know what you want

✅ GOOD
"I'm a student (15) and need 3 facts about climate change for my presentation. Please use simple language, bullet points."

Who, What, How – everything clear!

⚙️ The Secret: System Prompts

What is a system prompt?

A system prompt is like the game instructions you give the AI at the beginning. It stays active for the entire conversation.

🎭

Imagine telling an actor before the play: "You are a friendly teacher who uses simple words." The actor stays in that role – just like the AI.

For beginners:
"Always respond in simple language, as if you're talking to a 12-year-old. Use short sentences."
For experts:
"You are a physics professor. Respond in detail with technical terms."
For creatives:
"You are a creative writer. Respond with vivid imagery and metaphors."
🌍 LANGUAGE TIP

Greeting in both languages!

Compare the same greeting in German and English:

🇩🇪 Deutsch
"Ich bin Schüler, 15 Jahre. Brauche Hilfe bei Mathe. Bitte erkläre Schritt für Schritt."
VS
🇺🇸 English
"I'm a student, 15 years old. Need help with math. Please explain step by step."
💡

Observation: Both work well! But: English can often distinguish more subtly (e.g., "step by step" vs "explain simply"). Try both and remember what works better!

💪 Practice Tasks

1

Situation: You have to give a school speech about environmental protection.

"I'm a student (16) and need to give a 5-minute speech about environmental protection. Please give me 3 main points as bullet points, simple language, convincing."
2

Situation: You're studying for an English exam and need help with grammar.

"I'm studying for an English exam (level B1). Explain Past Perfect to me with simple examples and a memory rule."
3

Situation: You want to explain AI to a friend but don't fully understand it yourself.

"I need to explain AI to a friend but only half understand it myself. Please explain it to me first like I'm 10, then more detailed."
