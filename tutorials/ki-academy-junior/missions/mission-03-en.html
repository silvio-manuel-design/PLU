The Context Experiment

Imagine walking into a hair salon and just saying: "Cut." Without context, anything could happen – a buzz cut, a trim, or complete shave.

If you say: "I'm a student, need a well-groomed look for internship weeks, but don't want to look too adult. My hair is wavy and thick." – then the hairdresser knows exactly what to do.

AI works the same way. The more context you give, the more precise the result. Context isn't just important – it's king!

🧅 The 3 Layers of Context

1

The Base Layer

Who are you? (Student, professional, beginner...)

Example: "I'm a 16-year-old student..."
2

The Goal Layer

What exactly do you want to achieve? (Presentation, learning, creative...)

Example: "...need to give a 5-minute presentation..."
3

The Format Layer

What should the result look like? (Bullet points, prose, table...)

Example: "...as bullet points with simple language."

All 3 layers combined:

"I'm a 16-year-old student and need to give a 5-minute presentation about climate change. Please as bullet points with simple language, maximum 3 main points."

⚖️ With vs. Without Context

❌

Without Context

"Write about dogs"
What AI thinks:
  • "Which dogs?"
  • "Should it be informative or funny?"
  • "Who is this for?"
  • "How long should it be?"

→ Generic, often useless response

VS
✅

With Context

"I'm a 2nd semester veterinary student preparing for an exam. Need an overview of dog breeds with anatomical peculiarities. Please as table: Breed | Peculiarity | Relevance for vet. Maximum 5 breeds."
What AI understands:
  • "Ah, academic context!"
  • "Exam-relevant info needed"
  • "Table format desired"
  • "Specific number: 5 breeds"

→ Precise, immediately usable response

⚙️ System Prompts in Detail

The Difference: User Prompt vs. System Prompt

👤 User Prompt

Your concrete question or instruction

"Explain the rule of three to me"
One-time
→

⚙️ System Prompt

The framework for the whole conversation

"You are a patient math teacher for high school students. Always explain with everyday examples."
Stays active

Ready-to-use System Prompts to Copy

🎓 The Patient Teacher
"You are a patient teacher who explains complex topics simply. Use everyday examples and make sure I understand before you continue. Ask questions if something is unclear."
🔬 The Precise Scientist
"You are a precise scientist. Answer fact-based, cite sources when possible, and clearly distinguish between facts and theories. Mark uncertainties accordingly."
✨ The Creative Brainstormer
"You are a creative brainstorming partner. Think unconventionally, suggest multiple approaches, and encourage me to connect ideas. No idea is too crazy."
💼 The Efficient Coach
"You are an efficient coach. Give direct, honest feedback. Focus on solution-oriented answers. Avoid filler words and platitudes."
🌍 LANGUAGE TIP

Context details in both languages!

Some details can be expressed more precisely in one language:

🇩🇪 Deutsch
"Explain it like you'd explain it to a 10-year-old who has never heard of it."
VS
🇺🇸 English
"Explain it like I'm five (ELI5)"
💡

Observation: English often has shorter, more concise expressions (like "ELI5" = Explain Like I'm 5). German, on the other hand, can pack complex relationships into one sentence. Use the strengths of both languages!

🌍 Real Scenarios

📧

Scenario: Important Email

Situation: You need to write a difficult email to your boss.

Who: "I'm an intern in the marketing department..."
Goal: "...need to ask for deadline extension due to illness..."
Tone: "...polite but professional, not too apologetic..."
Format: "...short and concise, maximum 150 words."
🔧

Scenario: Debugging code

Situation: Your Python program isn't working.

Who: "I've been learning Python for 2 months..."
Goal: "...program should read and sort data from CSV..."
Problem: "...getting error 'Index out of range'..."
Format: "...explain the error and give corrected code."
🎨

Scenario: Creative Writing

Situation: You need inspiration for a story.

Who: "I write short stories as a hobby..."
Genre: "...science fiction with dystopian setting..."
Goal: "...need 3 character ideas with backstory..."
Style: "...dark but with glimmer of hope, like in 'Blade Runner'."
